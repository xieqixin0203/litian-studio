关于我们

立田工作室
| 本工作室背靠顺德建筑设计院股份有限公司，是一个尊重本土文化与语境，并呈现新时代建筑语言的建筑工作室。
工作室主要设计领域包括公司总部园区、建筑改造、城市历史街区更新商业综合体、度假酒店、学校建筑等，设计内容囊括规划设计、建筑设计、室内设计的前期概念方案到施工落地，专注设计在实际项目中的落地细节与效果。
目前我们保持小型工作室的规模，采用扁平化的工作方式，依托严谨且适时而动的设计方法，为客户提供高品质定制化的专业服务。|

LITIAN STUDIO
| LITIAN STUDIO is affiliated with Shunde Architectural Design Institute Co.,Ltd. Our studio upholds respect for local culture and context while expressing the architectural language of the new era.
The studio’s major design practices cover the renewal of urban historic districts, corporate headquarters, industrial parks, building renovation, commercial complexes, resort hotels, educational facilities, and other typologies. Its design services cover the entire process from preliminary conceptual planning, architectural design, and interior design to construction implementation, with a focus on the practical details and final outcomes of design in real-world projects.
Maintaining a compact studio scale and adopting a flat working structure, we rely on a responsive design methodology to deliver high-quality, customized professional services for our clients. |
